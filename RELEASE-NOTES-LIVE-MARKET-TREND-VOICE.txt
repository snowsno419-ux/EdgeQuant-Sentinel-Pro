EDGEQUANT SENTINEL - LIVE MARKET PRICE, MULTI-TIMEFRAME TREND & VOICE UPDATE

Dashboard additions
- Prominent XAUUSD mid-price display from the currently connected MT5 terminal.
- Bid and Ask remain visible; the displayed market price is explicitly the midpoint.
- Quote refresh is polled approximately every 1.5 seconds while Dashboard is active.
- M5, M15, H1 and H4 trend labels are calculated from MT5 candle history using EMA 20/50
  plus the latest close relative to EMA20. Trend history refreshes approximately every 20s.
- Trend labels are descriptive (BULL / BEAR / RANGE); insufficient or unavailable history
  is shown as unavailable rather than guessed.
- Added SPEAK MARKET UPDATE action. It reads the live quote and all four timeframe labels.
- Speech follows the existing Voice settings: Sinhala uses the configured Sinhala female
  Windows voice or the optional local Dialog/UoM Sinhala female VITS model; English uses
  the configured Windows voice path. Sinhala and English are selectable in Voice settings.
- Speech is generated on demand and is not an autonomous prediction. It states that the
  trend summary is technical context, not a guaranteed forecast or trade signal.

Safety and limitations
- Market data is read-only and comes from the connected MT5 terminal; no synthetic price
  or external price feed is substituted.
- If MT5 is disconnected or quotes/candles are stale/unavailable, the UI indicates this.
- Trend classification is a simple EMA-based technical description, not an AI forecast.
- Voice quality and actual audio playback must be listened to and verified on the user's
  Windows PC. Sinhala offline VITS requires the separately installed model/runtime.
- Existing order execution, spread guard, Manual Review, Telegram Studio and Auto STOP
  behavior are preserved by this change.
