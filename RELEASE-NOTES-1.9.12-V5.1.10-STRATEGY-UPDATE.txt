EdgeQuant Sentinel v1.9.12 — UI V5.1.10
2026-10-29

Owner-confirmed entry offset and opposite-position behavior update

- Entry offsets now apply across all channels: BUY entries move 10 pips down from the signal entry; SELL entries move 10 pips up. Examples: BUY 4039 -> 4038; SELL 4042 -> 4043, for gold at 0.1 per pip.
- The original signal SL remains exactly as posted. If the reversed offset makes the SL invalid for the order direction, the existing fail-closed SL validation still rejects that order.
- Removed the opposite-direction hedge blocker. Existing BUY positions alone no longer prevent a valid SELL signal and vice versa.
- Other safety checks remain: valid parse/SL/TP, freshness, TP-hit, channel enabled, account and broker selection, session/market, news, spread, per-symbol limits, LIVE confirmation, daily monetary loss and other risk controls. This change does not force malformed, stale, invalid or risk-blocked messages to trade.
- Layer 1 remains +49 pips; Layer 2 retains the existing selected signal TP moved 10 pips toward the adjusted entry. Two layers per entry remain.
- Exact-entry Telegram follow-up management from V5.1.9 remains intact; owner AUTO BE ON/OFF and trigger remain authoritative.
